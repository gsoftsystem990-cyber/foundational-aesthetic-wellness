Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who we are

Foundational Aesthetic Wellness (“we,” “us”) operates foundationalaestheticwellness.com and related booking, membership, review, and contact tools. Practice location: 399 Arcola Road, Suite 100, Collegeville, PA 19426. Phone: 267-281-8236.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Contact & booking: name, email, phone, service interest, preferred appointment time, and message text.
  • Membership enrollment: name, email, phone, date of birth, existing-patient status, optional chart/patient ID, plan selection, optional family-member names/ages, and optional notes. Card numbers are never collected on this website — payment is handled by Stripe Checkout.
  • Reviews: name, email, rating, and review text. Email is used for moderation only and is not shown publicly; approved name and review text may be published on the site.
  • Staff CRM (admin only): address, notes, and optional patient photo uploaded by staff.

Please do not submit clinical diagnoses, full medical records, or payment card numbers in free-text fields. Do not include children’s personal information unless it is necessary for a family membership request and you are the parent or legal guardian.

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule or confirm appointments
  • Process membership enrollment and renewals
  • Moderate and display approved patient reviews
  • Operate the staff admin tools for the practice
  • Improve site reliability and prevent abuse (rate limits, security logs)

Processors and sharing

We use service providers to run the site. They process data only as needed to provide their services:

  • Stripe: payment processing for memberships (email and limited membership metadata). Card data is collected and stored by Stripe, not by us.
  • WhatsApp / Meta: if configured, appointment confirmation messages may be sent to the phone number you provide.
  • Google Fonts: fonts may be loaded from Google’s servers when you view pages.
  • Hosting provider: the company that hosts this website and database stores data on secured servers you choose for production.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the practice, patients, or the public.

Cookies and local storage

Essential cookies are used for staff admin sign-in (httpOnly session cookie). The public marketing site does not require cookies for browsing. The browser may store limited local data for review display preferences. We do not use advertising trackers on this site today.

Retention

Contact messages, booking requests, membership records, reviews, and patient CRM data are retained while needed for care coordination, membership administration, and legitimate business records, then deleted or anonymized when no longer required. You may request deletion of your website submissions by contacting the practice; some records may be retained if required for legal, financial, or safety reasons.

Security

We use HTTPS in production, access controls for staff admin, hashed admin passwords, rate limiting, and input validation. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. This website is an operations and marketing system; it is not a substitute for a certified electronic health record, and we do not claim HIPAA certification for this site alone.

Your choices

  • Email or call us to update or delete information you submitted through the website
  • Cancel membership with 30 days’ notice per membership terms
  • Ask that an approved review be removed from the public site

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through online forms. Family membership fields should be completed only by a parent or guardian when relevant.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact
