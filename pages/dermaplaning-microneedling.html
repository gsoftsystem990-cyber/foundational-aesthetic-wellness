Dermaplaning & Microneedling

Reveal smoother, brighter, and more youthful-looking skin with our advanced skin rejuvenation treatments. Dermaplaning and microneedling are two highly effective, non-invasive procedures designed to improve skin texture, tone, and overall radiance.

Dermaplaning

Dermaplaning is a precision skincare technique that uses a sterile surgical blade to gently exfoliate the skin's surface. This process removes dead skin cells and fine vellus hair (peach fuzz), instantly brightening the complexion and smoothing uneven texture.

Benefits of Dermaplaning

  • Enhances skincare product absorption for better results
  • Creates a smooth base for flawless makeup application
  • Encourages cell turnover for a more radiant appearance
  • Noticeable glow with minimal downtime

Signs Your Skin May Benefit

  • Dull, lackluster skin or uneven texture
  • Peach fuzz (vellus hair) or mild hyperpigmentation
  • Poor product absorption or makeup not applying smoothly
  • Visible fine lines and early signs of aging

Recommended frequency: Most patients benefit from treatments every 3–4 weeks.

Microneedling

Microneedling uses fine, medical-grade needles to create controlled micro-injuries in the skin, stimulating collagen and elastin production. This helps improve firmness, texture, and overall skin tone.

Benefits of Microneedling

  • Improves firmness and elasticity through collagen stimulation
  • Reduces the appearance of acne scars and rough texture
  • Revives dull, dehydrated skin
  • Boosts effectiveness of your skincare routine
  • Quick recovery with mild redness after treatment

Signs Your Skin May Benefit

  • Fine lines, wrinkles, or loss of firmness
  • Acne scars, large pores, or uneven texture
  • Hyperpigmentation, sun damage, or dullness
  • Skincare products not absorbing well

Recommended frequency: A series of 3–6 treatments spaced 4–6 weeks apart, followed by maintenance sessions every few months.
