Laser Esthetics & Snoring Solutions

Non-invasive treatments for confidence and better sleep.

From skin rejuvenation and lip enhancement to laser hair removal and snoring therapy, Foundational Aesthetic Wellness offers advanced laser esthetic solutions tailored to your goals. Every treatment begins with a personalized consultation.

Skin Rejuvenation & Acne Care

  • Fotona Micropeel — A gentle fractionated laser treatment that improves fine lines, skin texture, sun damage, and pigmentation with minimal downtime.
  • Micro Laser Peel — Active acne treatment for clearer, healthier-looking skin.
  • Skin Resurfacing — Restore a smoother, more even complexion.

Lip Enhancement — Fotona LipLase

A non-invasive treatment that enhances lip volume naturally by stimulating collagen production.

  • Plumps and smooths lips without injectables
  • Enhances shape and definition
  • Recommended: 4 weekly sessions plus maintenance every 3–4 months

Eye Rejuvenation — Fotona SmoothEye

A gentle laser treatment designed to firm and tighten the delicate eye area.

  • Lifts upper and lower eyelids
  • Reduces fine lines and wrinkles around the eyes
  • Minimal discomfort and downtime — typically 3 treatments for optimal results

Laser Hair Removal

Permanent hair reduction using advanced laser technology. Laser energy targets hair follicles to reduce regrowth over time.

  • Quick sessions ranging from minutes to about an hour
  • Multiple sessions recommended for best long-term results
  • Finer, lighter regrowth over time

NightLase® Snoring Therapy

A non-invasive laser treatment designed to reduce snoring and improve sleep quality. Laser energy gently tightens airway tissues, helping keep the airway open during sleep.

  • No devices or masks required
  • Comfortable, quick sessions with no downtime
  • Results can last up to a year
  • Ideal for patients who cannot tolerate CPAP or oral appliances

What to Expect

  • Personalized consultation to determine the best treatment plan
  • Avoid sun exposure before and after treatment as directed
  • Follow customized pre- and post-care instructions
  • Some treatments may require a series of sessions for optimal results
