Wellness Advantage Plan

An in-office membership (not insurance) for patients who want simpler pricing and automatic yearly renewal. Enroll online; your card is entered only on Stripe’s checkout page.

How the plan works

1. Sign up

Tell us who you are so we can match you to an existing chart when possible. No insurance required.

2. Pay on Stripe

You leave this site and enter card details on Stripe Checkout. We never see or store your full card number.

3. Save immediately

Membership is active after payment. Front desk applies member pricing on eligible visits.

4. Auto-renew

Stripe bills the same plan each year until you cancel. Failed cards are retried; we are notified if membership lapses.

Enroll

Payment security: this form collects identity only (name, date of birth, phone). Card number, CVV, and expiry are collected by Stripe, not by this website.

By continuing you agree this is a practice membership / discount plan, not dental or medical insurance. You will complete payment on Stripe. Membership renews annually until canceled.
