About Foundational Aesthetic Wellness

Welcome to Foundational Aesthetic Wellness located in Devon on the Main Line in the Philadelphia area. We specialize in advanced laser esthetics, skin rejuvenation, and non-invasive cosmetic treatments tailored to your goals.

Led by Medical Licensed Esthetician and Certified Laser Technician Lexi Lojewski, our practice focuses on personalized, results-driven care — from LipLase and EyeLase™ to dermaplaning and microneedling. We concentrate on your well-being and goals ahead of all else, creating a luxurious experience that builds long-term confidence and radiant skin.
