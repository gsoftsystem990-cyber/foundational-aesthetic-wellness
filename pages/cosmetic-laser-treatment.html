Cosmetic Laser Treatment

Foundational Aesthetic Wellness offers advanced cosmetic laser treatments using the Fotona LightWalker laser — one of the most trusted systems in the industry. Laser technology allows us to deliver precise, comfortable treatments with stunning results and minimal downtime.

Our laser treatments are less invasive than traditional procedures and are ideal for patients who want effective results without needles or lengthy recovery.

Benefits of Cosmetic Laser Treatment

  • High-precision, tissue-selective treatments
  • Less invasive and safer than many alternatives
  • Greater comfort during and after treatment
  • Immediate skin rejuvenation, tightening, and glow
  • Improved skin complexion with little or no downtime
  • No surgery, needles, or anesthesia required

Facial Treatments

  • Acne & Acne Scar Revision — Target active breakouts and improve the appearance of acne scarring.
  • LipLase™ — Non-invasive lip plumping for fuller lips without injectables.
  • SmoothEye® — Non-ablative tightening for the delicate periocular (eye) region.
  • EyeLase™ — Non-surgical eye lift and under-eye bag reduction with little to no downtime.

Body Treatments

  • Vascular Lesions — Address visible veins and vascular concerns on the skin.
  • Photobiomodulation & Pain Management — Support healing and comfort with targeted laser energy.

Every treatment begins with a personalized consultation to determine the best approach for your skin goals. Individual results may vary.
