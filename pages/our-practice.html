About Our Facility

Foundational Aesthetic Wellness is located in Collegeville, PA. Our team is proud to provide advanced laser esthetics, skin rejuvenation, and non-invasive cosmetic care for patients with diverse skin goals.

We specialize in personalized treatment plans — from LipLase and EyeLase™ to dermaplaning, microneedling, and laser hair removal — designed around your unique concerns and how to best reach them.

Our clinic was designed to provide a warm and welcoming environment. From the moment you arrive, our team will welcome you, prepare you for your visit, and create a luxurious, results-driven experience focused on healthy, radiant skin and long-term confidence.
