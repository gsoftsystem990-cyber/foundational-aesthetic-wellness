About Our Facility

Foundational Aesthetic Wellness has a premier location in Devon on the Main Line in the Philadelphia area. We provide advanced laser esthetics, skin rejuvenation, and non-invasive cosmetic care in a warm, welcoming environment.

Led by Lexi Lojewski, Medical Licensed Esthetician and Certified Laser Technician, our practice specializes in personalized treatments including LipLase, EyeLase™, dermaplaning, and microneedling — carefully tailored to your skin goals.

Our clinic was designed so we can focus on your unique concerns and how to best reach them. From the moment you arrive, our team will welcome you, prepare you for your visit, and create a luxurious, results-driven experience centered on healthy, radiant skin and long-term confidence.
