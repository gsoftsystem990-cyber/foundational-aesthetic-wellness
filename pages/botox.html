Botox®

Softened lines. A refreshed, natural expression.

Botox® temporarily relaxes the muscles that cause dynamic wrinkles — such as frown lines, crow’s feet, and forehead creases — helping you look rested without looking frozen.

Benefits

  • Softens fine lines and expression wrinkles
  • Quick treatment with little to no downtime
  • Subtle, customized dosing for natural results
  • Results typically begin within a few days and last several months

What to Expect

During your consultation, we review your goals and facial movement patterns to create a personalized plan. Treatment is typically brief, and most patients return to normal activities the same day.
