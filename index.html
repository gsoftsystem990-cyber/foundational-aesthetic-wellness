Meet Your Esthetician

Lexi Lojewski

Medical Licensed Esthetician & Certified Laser Technician

Alexis is a Medical Licensed Esthetician and Certified Laser Technician with a passion for results-driven, advanced skincare. She began her academic journey at West Chester University, where she studied Sports Medicine, before pursuing her true calling in aesthetics at Lansdale School of Cosmetology, where she earned her esthetics license.

She further refined her expertise at the Institute for Laser Medicine, with advanced certifications in laser hair removal, laser skin resurfacing, and photofacials, along with dermaplaning and microneedling. Known for her detail-oriented approach and genuine care, Lexi creates a luxurious, personalized experience for every client.
